    Granny & Mundiya

    Four levels. Two hunters. Five days. Every time they catch you, the asylum rearranges itself and locks down tighter.

    • 5 days. Each capture burns a day, shuffles every item and raises security.
    • 3-slot satchel. Keys, tools, fuses and relics all compete for the same hands.
    • Granny patrols and hunts by sound. Sprint, drop something, creak a floorboard — she comes.
    • Mundiya is silent, walks through walls, and follows your light.
